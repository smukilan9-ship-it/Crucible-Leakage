Reverie Hacks 2026 · ML Prompt Engineering

Anyone can ask a model once. This is what it takes to trust the answer.

Hand a model the column names, ask which ones leak, take the answer. That was the first version of this, and it is right most of the time. The trouble is that you cannot tell which times. Six things follow from that, and each one is a measurement rather than an opinion.

What the leakage was worth

On Titanic, your model scored 0.175 macro F1 higher than it deserved to.

Two columns were reading the answer rather than predicting it: boat, the lifeboat a passenger was put into, and body, the number given to a recovered corpse. Both are written down after the ship goes under. Every figure below compares the same models fitted with and without them.

01  what one call cannot do

You cannot tell a firm answer from a shaky one

0.380how far F1 moves between two shuffles of one prompt
Ask once

One call is one draw from a distribution you never get to see.

What this does instead

The same question goes in once per shuffled column order, and how far the answers move is kept as a number instead of thrown away.

02  what one call cannot do

Nothing outside the model has looked

0.013the lifeboat column's correlation with survival
Ask once

The model marks its own work, and it is right often enough that you stop checking it.

What this does instead

A screen with no model in it runs alongside and fails in the opposite direction. The two of them disagreeing is the finding.

03  what one call cannot do

One call cannot tell you it was unsure

4columns the passes could not agree on, every one reported with both arguments instead of a verdict
Ask once

Ask once and you get one side of the argument, stated with total confidence, and no sign that an argument existed.

What this does instead

Ask three times and the disagreement surfaces. On the dropout table four columns split, and both readings are printed side by side rather than settled by a majority of three.

04  what one call cannot do

There is no sentence a reviewer can check

2admissible records in 30,136 swept for documented leakage
Ask once

You get a verdict and a feeling.

What this does instead

Every finding carries the published sentence it rests on and where to find it. Where there is none, it says so rather than filling the gap.

05  what one call cannot do

Nobody with judgement is in the loop

16%of columns reach a person, holding 40 of the 40 known leaks
Ask once

Unless they re-read all 306 columns, which is the work they were trying to avoid.

What this does instead

A short list, ordered by disagreement. Nothing is removed until a person says so.

06  what one call cannot do

It never finds out whether it was right

0.974what a correlation filter scores. The leaking model scored 0.972.
Ask once

There is no number for it to be wrong about.

What this does instead

The model is refit with the columns and without them on the same folds, next to an arm holding whatever a correlation filter would have left.
